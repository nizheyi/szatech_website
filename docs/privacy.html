Privacy and analytics

Our inquiry form asks for a reply email and a description of your requirement. Optional fields include your name, company or laboratory, quantity and target timing. Formspree processes the information you submit so SZATech can receive and respond to your inquiry. Please send drawings by email only when you choose to do so.

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 helps us understand traffic sources, page and case views, and contact actions. We do not send form fields, inquiry text, drawings, email addresses or full email links as analytics events. We remove arbitrary URL query strings from the page address sent by our measurement code. The analytics tag is not loaded until you choose “Allow analytics.” Declining does not affect the inquiry form.

You can change your choice using “Analytics settings” in the page footer. If you withdraw consent, the page reloads without the analytics tag. Search Console uses Google's search data and does not require a tracking tag on your visit.

For a question about information you sent us, contact steven@szatech.com.
